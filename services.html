AFTERFALL ARCHITECT

Our own mapping toolkit, designed and continually developed by Afterfall Studio to expand creative possibilities inside DayZ Editor.

ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT — Features are being implemented and tested progressively.

MEASUREMENT TOOL

A dedicated measurement tool intended to help plan distances, alignments and spatial layouts accurately. Development in progress.

▦

OBJECT PLACEMENT BRUSHES

Brushes for placing environmental objects and detailing zones faster, including debris and vegetation. Features are being integrated and refined.

☀

ENVIRONMENT CONTROLS

Dedicated interface controls for day/night and weather to assist visual checks while editing. Implementation and validation are ongoing.
